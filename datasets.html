daily-updating open datasets

Open data that refreshes itself every day — no signup, no key. Most "live" dashboards hoard their history; these publish it. Curated by the trendscope project.
Bring your own reader: our full source list as trendscope.opml — 2380+ feeds in one file, importable into Feedly, NetNewsWire, FreshRSS or any OPML reader. Regenerated daily by CI, same CC0 license as the data.
