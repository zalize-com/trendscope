Working notes for the W47 digest. Each entry: the signal, where it showed up,
and what would confirm/kill it as a trend. Delete or fold into the weekly
notes as evidence lands.

## 1. AI career anxiety is surfacing as a genre
- "What are the most useful, concrete AI skills to independently learn?"
  (82 pts, 51 comments)
- "My product makes money, AI does the coding, and I feel useless" (5 pts,
  early)
- "Do I own the code I write with the help of AI?" (4 pts)
- "Do you still need to know what's going on in the code?" (2 pts)
- **Confirm if**: more identity/ownership/skills threads hit the board this
  week, or a major post (not Ask) covers "developer purpose post-AI".
- **Framing for notes**: the question has shifted from "can AI write code"
  to "what's left for me" — watch for the tooling/infra answers (evals,
  verification, review tooling) that follow existential threads.
