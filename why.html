what "keeping every day" buys you

  • Velocity, not just rank — gainers/droppers/new entries published daily (compare, movers API).
  • Streaks & staying power — which stories last a week, not a moment (streaks, this week).
  • Time travel — replay any day's board (dashboard date picker, calendar).
  • The canon — 19 years of Hacker News history backfilled: front pages, launches, questions, by year.
  • Demand signals — recurring Ask HN themes clustered (ideas) and the HN labor market (hiring pulse, 15-year series).
  • Self-described health — the pipeline publishes its own uptime (health).
